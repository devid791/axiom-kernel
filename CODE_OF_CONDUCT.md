# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Axiom Kernel project a
welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and technically precise.
- Assume good faith and discuss code, evidence and trade-offs.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them openly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing,
publication of private information, and disruptive or abusive conduct are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/devid791). Do not include
credentials, private endpoints or production data in a report.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation
when necessary to protect the project community.
